**📌 Project Overview**

TradeZone is a simulated e-commerce analytics project designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, seller efficiency, product trends, and operational insights using SQL Server and Power BI.

The project focuses on transforming raw transactional data into actionable business insights through:

-Data cleaning and validation

-SQL-based business analysis

-Interactive Power BI dashboards

-Executive-level reporting and storytelling

This project demonstrates practical skills in:

-Data Analytics
-SQL Querying
-Data Modeling
-Business Intelligence
-Dashboard Design
-Insight Communication

**🎯 Project Objectives**

The major objectives of this project were to:

-Analyze revenue performance across states and cities
-Measure customer acquisition and conversion trends
-Evaluate seller performance using ratings and order metrics
-Identify top-performing product categories
-Study payment behavior and customer segmentation
-Build an interactive dashboard for decision-making

**🛠 Tools & Technologies Used**
-Microsoft SQL Server
-Microsoft Power BI

The project consists of multiple related tables:

-Main Tables
-customers
-orders
-order_items
-products
-sellers
-payments
-reviews

**🧹 Data Cleaning & Validation**

Data cleaning was performed using SQL and Power Query.

Cleaning Tasks Included:

-Business rule enforcement

**📊 Key Business Questions Answered**

The analysis focused on answering critical business questions such as:

-Which states generate the highest revenue?
-What product categories drive the most sales?
-Which sellers perform best based on ratings?
-How do payment methods vary across customers?
-What customer segments contribute most revenue?
-How does Average Order Value (AOV) vary by city?
-What trends exist in monthly order volume?
-What factors affect customer conversion?

💡 Key Insights

Some major findings from the analysis include:

-Lagos generated the highest share of total revenue
-Electronics contributed significantly to revenue growth
-Card payments were the dominant payment method
-Order volume increased toward the end of the year
-High-rated sellers generally performed better financially
-Revenue concentration indicated dependency on a few major categories
-Similar purchase quantities across categories suggest demand consistency rather than product underperformance.

**🚀 Recommendations**

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were proposed:

-Improve onboarding to increase customer conversion
-Monitor underperforming sellers
-Diversify product offerings
-Optimize marketing in high-performing regions
-Encourage digital payment adoption
-Improve customer retention strategies
